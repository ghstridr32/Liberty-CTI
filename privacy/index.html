1. Information You Provide

Liberty CTI collects information you choose to give us directly, including:

  • Alamo Threat Brief registration: full name, work email address, organization, role/title, and industry sector, plus whether you opt in to receive the free weekly executive summary and Liberty CTI updates.
  • Sign-in: the email address you use to sign back in to a registered account.
  • Contact form: full name, organization, email address, and the content of your message.

We do not currently collect payment information, account passwords, or government identification through the Services.

2. Automatically Collected Information

Liberty CTI does not run third-party analytics or advertising trackers on the website. Beyond the standard server and content-delivery logs generated by our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) — which may include IP address, browser and device information, and request timestamps for security and reliability purposes — the site does not automatically collect additional behavioral or analytics data about visitors.

When you register for Alamo Threat Brief access, our system additionally records the page you registered from and, where provided by your browser, the referring page, as part of the registration record described below.

3. ATB Reader Tracking

Liberty CTI may record when a registered reader's account was created, last used to sign in, and updated, in order to administer Alamo Threat Brief access. We do not currently track which individual ATB issues a registered reader has opened; if Liberty CTI begins recording issue-level readership in the future, this policy will be updated to describe that practice before it takes effect.

4. Purposes of Processing

Liberty CTI uses the information described above to:

  • Administer Alamo Threat Brief reader accounts and grant access to full issues;
  • Respond to inquiries submitted through the contact form;
  • Send the free weekly executive summary and Liberty CTI updates to readers who opt in;
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the Services and prevent abuse (for example, the registration form's spam-prevention safeguard); and
  • Improve Liberty CTI's products over time.

5. Vendors & Service Providers

Liberty CTI uses a limited number of third-party service providers to operate the Services:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, content delivery, and the Workers KV storage that holds Alamo Threat Brief registration records.
  • Brevo — email delivery, used only for readers who opt in to receive the free weekly executive summary and Liberty CTI updates.
  • Formspree — processes submissions from the website contact form.

Each provider processes information only as necessary to perform these functions on Liberty CTI's behalf.

6. Cookies & Similar Technologies

The Services use one functional cookie: a signed session cookie (lcti_atb_access) that keeps a registered reader signed in to Alamo Threat Brief access for up to 180 days. This cookie is set only after registration or sign-in, is used solely to maintain your session, and is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. Liberty CTI does not currently use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies.

7. Data Retention

Liberty CTI retains registration and contact information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain reader accounts, and meet legitimate business and legal needs. Liberty CTI is developing a formal internal data retention schedule as the business grows.

8. Data Security

Liberty CTI applies reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect the information we hold, including signed, HTTP-only session cookies and access controls on our hosting infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and Liberty CTI cannot guarantee absolute, "military-grade," or breach-proof security under all circumstances.

9. Your Choices

  • Marketing communications: you may decline or later stop the free weekly executive summary and Liberty CTI updates by not opting in at registration, or by contacting Liberty CTI to be removed.
  • Account information: contact Liberty CTI to update or correct your registration information.
  • Account deletion: contact Liberty CTI to request deletion of your registration record.
  • General privacy questions: contact Liberty CTI at any time using the details below.

10. Texas Privacy Considerations

Liberty CTI is based in San Antonio, Texas. As Liberty CTI's business grows, we intend to structure our data practices to align with the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) and other applicable Texas privacy law. This Policy does not represent a present claim of full TDPSA compliance; Liberty CTI will update this section as our practices and legal obligations evolve.

11. Children

The Services are intended for business and professional users and are not directed to children. Liberty CTI does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Policy Updates

Liberty CTI may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the date of the most recent material revision.

Questions about this Policy can be sent to intel@libertycti.com.
