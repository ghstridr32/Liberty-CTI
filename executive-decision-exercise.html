Executive Decision Exercise

Find the decision gaps before the crisis does.

A 90-minute facilitated exercise that places your leadership team inside a realistic cyber and geopolitical crisis to show where warning, authority, information, and decision-making break down under pressure.

Built on Your Baseline

We don't start with your technology. We start with your decisions.

The Exercise is the second half of the Executive Decision Readiness Engagement. It follows the Executive Intelligence Baseline, which establishes your important decisions, their owners, your outside dependencies, warning conditions, and escalation thresholds.

That is what makes the scenario yours. Without the Baseline, an exercise tests a generic organization. With it, the exercise tests the decisions your leadership will actually face.

What the Exercise Tests

The scenario is simulated. The decisions are not.

Warning

What did leadership know, and when?

Authority

Who could actually make the call?

Dependencies

What outside your organization changed the decision?

Intelligence

What could you have known earlier that would have changed the outcome?

Areas tested in every exercise: authorities · information flow · escalation · continuity decisions · communications · third-party dependencies · government coordination · governance · documentation · leadership judgment under uncertainty.

What Happens

A fast executive exercise with a documented outcome.

Before

The Baseline sets the scenario

Objectives, participants, authorities, assumptions, and information-handling rules are agreed in advance. Injects are tied to the decision points the Baseline identified.

During

Ninety minutes, facilitated

Liberty CTI principals run a decision-driven scenario. Decisions, owners, dissent, deferred choices, and information requests are recorded as they happen.

After

Findings leadership can use

A decision record, tested strengths, material decision gaps, maturity findings, prioritized actions, and the governance records worth preserving.

The goal is not theater. The goal is to find the decision, warning, governance, and dependency gaps before a real incident exposes them.

Who Should Participate

The right room is the leadership room.

  • CEO
  • COO
  • General Counsel
  • CISO
  • CIO
  • Chief Risk Officer
  • Operations
  • Government Affairs
  • Board risk committee

IT and security leaders take part as essential advisors. The exercise keeps board and executive decisions visible; it does not test technical response.

Who Runs It

Not someone reading a facilitator guide.

Liberty CTI's exercises are personally designed and facilitated by principals with decades of military, intelligence, cyber, and national-security experience, including the White House Situation Room, the Pentagon, and cyber intelligence command. They have worked where information was incomplete, time was short, senior leaders needed answers, and the cost of a wrong judgment was real.

They know the difference between receiving information and receiving the information that changes a decision.

Your first crisis shouldn't be the first time your leadership team makes these decisions.

The Exercise is delivered as part of the Executive Decision Readiness Engagement, together with the Baseline that makes it specific to you.
