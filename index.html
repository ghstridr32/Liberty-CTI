Executive Decision Maturity

Know where your decision process is today. Move it one meaningful level higher.

Most organizations already have security tools, technical teams, and incident plans. Fewer can show that senior leaders recognize a changing external threat, understand its consequence, decide in time, and document the decision responsibly.

  1. 1

    Ad Hoc

    Decisions depend heavily on individuals and circumstances.

  2. 2

    Repeatable

    Familiar practices exist, but ownership, thresholds, escalation, and documentation remain inconsistent.

  3. 3

    Defined

    Decision rights, warning requirements, escalation triggers, and governance records are established.

  4. 4

    Decision-Driven

    External warning is integrated into leadership routines. Decisions are timely, rehearsed, and documented.

  5. 5

    Adaptive

    Leadership systematically learns from exercises, real events, prior judgments, changed assumptions, and outcomes.

The goal is not Level 5 for everyone. It is the next level that matters for your decisions. How we use the model

Cascade Intelligence

Texas operates as a coupled system.

Energy, data centers, finance, healthcare, and defense production do not fail in isolation. Liberty CTI tracks how pressure in one system can move into decisions in another.

  1. EnergyERCOT, generation, fuel, and grid operations
  2. Data Centers & CloudPower-dependent compute that every sector now shares
  3. FinancePayments, liquidity, and vendor concentration
  4. HealthcareClinical availability, claims, and patient diversion
  5. Defense Industrial BasePrograms, subcontractors, and mission dependencies

Not every event follows one fixed chain. The point is that executive decisions must account for dependencies outside the organization, before a disruption somewhere else becomes a decision here.
