The Adversaries

Who Is Targeting Texas

Texas is the 8th largest economy in the world. It is under active, sustained attack from the world's most capable cyber adversaries. Liberty CTI tracks them all.

China — PRC
MSS/PLA cyber espionage targeting energy, defense, and advanced technology. Long-term strategic collection on Texas OT/ICS infrastructure and semiconductor supply chains.
Russia — SVR/GRU/FSB
Critical infrastructure pre-positioning and ransomware ecosystem support. Targeting ERCOT-connected energy networks and Texas financial sector.
Iran — IRGC/APT
Disruptive hacktivist proxies and wiper attacks on energy and healthcare. Increased Texas-focused activity linked to geopolitical tensions.
North Korea — DPRK
Financially-motivated cyber operations targeting financial institutions. Cryptocurrency theft and defense contractor IP theft supporting WMD programs.
Transnational Cybercrime
Ransomware-as-a-service ecosystems enabling attacks on hospitals, municipalities, and industrial targets across Texas.
